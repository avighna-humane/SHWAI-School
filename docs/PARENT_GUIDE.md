# SHWAI parent and guardian guide

Parents and guardians receive access only through an active, school-scoped membership linked to persisted parent and student records. A parent cannot gain access by submitting an arbitrary student ID; linking must be performed through an authorized invitation or school workflow.

## Available family workflows

The parent portal is designed for linked children only. Depending on the configured school data and permissions, it can expose published grades, attendance, homework, approved reports, meetings, notices, notifications, and approved learning support. Private tutor conversations and unrelated students remain outside the parent view.

## Communication and consent

Email invitations, verification, recovery, SMS, WhatsApp, and push delivery require configured providers and consent/preferences. The application records in-app notification state through school- and recipient-scoped server actions. It does not claim that an external message was delivered merely because a provider request was attempted.

## Privacy

Parents should use the school’s authorized privacy and support channels for correction, access, export, or deletion requests. Retention, legal holds, jurisdiction-specific consent, and destructive deletion require school policy and deployment verification; this repository does not claim regulatory certification.

## Current limitations

Live provider delivery, production storage downloads, authenticated browser evidence, and jurisdiction-specific legal review remain required before a real school relies on these workflows.
