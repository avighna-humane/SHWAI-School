## 2. REMOVE ROLE SWITCHING

Completely remove the role-switching functionality/dropdown from the authenticated dashboard.

A logged-in user must NOT be able to switch roles from inside the dashboard.

- Switch role
- Change role
- Switch account type
- Role selector
- Administrator/Principal switching
- Student/Parent switching

The user's dashboard and permissions should be determined by their authenticated account/role.

The only place where a user chooses their role should be during the initial login/entry flow.

The user-facing role structure is:

SCHOOL
- Principal
- Teacher
- Owner

STUDENT
- Student
- Parent

Do NOT show Administrator as a separate user-facing role. Principal represents the school administration role.

- Allow the parent to select/view their different children where appropriate.
- This is NOT role switching.
- Keep the parent logged in as Parent while changing which child they are viewing.

Do not create unnecessary duplicate parent accounts.
